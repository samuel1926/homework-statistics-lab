EXERCISE / 01 · THEORY

What does independence mean?

In probability, independence is a property of the joint distribution. In statistics, we observe a finite sample and ask whether the available evidence is compatible with independence in the underlying population.

PPROBABILITY · EXACT DEFINITION

Independence of random variables

P(X = x, F = f) = P(X = x) P(F = f)

For every possible pair of values, the joint probability factors into the two marginal probabilities. Equivalently, when P(X=x) > 0, P(F=f | X=x) = P(F=f): learning X does not change the distribution of F.

Population propertyAll pairs (x, f)

From observations to frequencies

p̂ij = Oij / n  ;  p̂j|i = Oij / ni·

The contingency table collects observed counts Oij. Row and column totals yield marginal frequencies; dividing each cell by its row or column total yields conditional frequencies.

Eij = ni· × n·jn

If the variables are independent, multiply the row total by the column total and divide by the sample size to obtain the expected cell count Eij.

PEARSON CHI-SQUARE STATISTICχ² = Σi,j (Oij − Eij)² / EijDegrees of freedom = (r − 1)(c − 1). Larger values indicate greater discrepancies between observed and independence-expected counts.
ⓘ
Two different questions. Exact independence means a population distribution factors for every category pair. A χ² test does not prove that property: it tests the null hypothesis using sample counts, a chosen significance level α and an asymptotic approximation.

RESEARCH NOTES

What the evidence can tell us.

A statistical test is not a machine that establishes mathematical independence or explains why two variables are related. Interpret the result at the right level.

01 / STATISTICAL INFERENCE

Reject or fail to reject

If p < α, reject the null hypothesis of independence: the data provide evidence of association. If p ≥ α, do not reject: the observed sample is compatible with independence, but this does not prove independence.

02 / EFFECT SIZE & ASSUMPTIONS

Check the whole table

χ² is affected by the sample size. Cramér's V summarizes strength on a 0–1 scale. The usual χ² p-value uses a large-sample approximation; sparse expected cells require caution or a different method.

03 / ASSOCIATION ≠ CAUSATION

Don't infer a mechanism

An association may reflect a causal influence, reverse causality, confounding or selection effects. Observational contingency tables alone cannot establish which variable causes another or whether either causes anything.

Method, reproducibility & references

Tables and statistics are computed locally from the dataset you provide. The p-value is the upper-tail probability of a chi-square distribution with (r−1)(c−1) degrees of freedom, implemented with the regularized incomplete gamma function. Example datasets are synthetic; there are no causal claims. Analyses are descriptive unless the observations are an appropriate sample for population inference.
